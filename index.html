FAQ

Questions
parents
ask first.

Is this a substitute for child psychology? ↓
No. BehaviourScope™ is an interpretive western-astrology lens designed to spark reflection, not diagnose. For clinical concerns, please consult a qualified child psychologist.
Who is Edusense built for? ↓
Parents of children aged 3-14, teachers wanting to understand a student's natural orientation, and principals running a value-added service for their school families.
Which languages are supported? ↓
English and हिंदी today, with Marathi on the roadmap. Toggle on the reading page.
Is my child's data safe? ↓
Birth details are stored only against your account. We don't share or sell child data. Delete any reading from your dashboard at any time.
How is this "research-backed"? ↓
Sentiment Lab implements Dr Bhawna Tiwari's PhD thesis "Sentimental Analysis approach to improve teaching and learning in primary education" - in plain English a parent can act on.
Can I try without paying? ↓
Yes. Free plan supports 1 child and unlimited BehaviourScope™ readings, forever. No credit card needed.
